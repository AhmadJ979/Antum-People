# Antum People — Pilot Anchor Outreach Messages (Ahmad's HR network)

> **Purpose:** Fill the 3 free anchor pilot slots via founder-led warm outreach.
> **Audience:** GCC HR / People leaders at mid-market firms (100–1,000 employees), UAE & KSA.
> **Sender:** Ahmad (founder).
>
> **Placeholders:** `[Name]`, `[Company]`, `[mutual contact]`, `[day/time]`, `[quarter]`.
>
> **Tone guardrails (non-negotiable):**
> - Outcome-led and specific, never hype. No invented metrics or guarantees.
> - No pressure tactics. "Three free slots" is stated as a fact, not "act now / only 2 left."
> - Honest about the exchange: free full access **in return for** feedback + testimonial + case-study rights.
> - Templates are drafts — mention "counsel sign-off" where templates come up.
> - Always leave an easy out ("no obligation", "no pressure either way", "happy to leave it with you").
> - EN by default; optional Arabic line for KSA contacts is included inline.

---

## 1. Warm ask for intro (Ahmad → connector)

**Goal:** get the connector to forward a blurb or make an intro. Two parts: the ask, and a forwardable blurb.

### 1a. The ask (~95 words)
> Hi [Name] — quick ask. I'm opening three free pilot slots for Antum People, the GCC onboarding/offboarding platform I've been building. It turns EOSB and PDPL compliance into a dashboard leadership actually reads — retention, time-to-productivity, and EOSB liability in one place. If you know an HR or People leader running a 100–1,000 person team in the UAE or KSA who's tired of spreadsheets and compliance fire drills, would you be open to an intro? I can send you a two-line blurb you forward as-is. No pressure either way.

### 1b. Forwardable blurb (~70 words)
> Antum People helps GCC HR teams (100–1,000 people) turn onboarding and offboarding into a leadership dashboard — correct EOSB (UAE basic-salary vs KSA total-salary), PDPL-compliant consent, and clear retention and time-to-value metrics. We're running three free pilot slots (full access, no cost) in exchange for feedback and a case study. Want a 30-minute look?

## Honest-caveat reminders (for the sender, not pasted into every message)

1. **Pilot is an exchange, not a giveaway.** Always name the ask: feedback + testimonial + case-study rights. Never imply "free forever."
2. **Templates need counsel sign-off.** If privacy notice / DPA / DPIA come up, say "drafts for your counsel to review" — never "approved."
3. **No performance guarantees.** We say we *measure* Retention Lift, Time-to-Value, and Compliance Accuracy — we do **not** promise a specific % improvement before we've run the pilot.
4. **Regulator integrations are roadmap.** MoHRE/Qiwa are workflow steps today, not live API push. Don't overclaim.
5. **Three slots is real, not urgency.** Say "three free slots" as fact; never "only 3 left — act fast."

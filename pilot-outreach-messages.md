# Antum People — Pilot Anchor Outreach Messages (Ahmad's HR network)

> **Purpose:** Fill the 3 free anchor pilot slots via founder-led warm outreach.
> **Audience:** UAE HR / People leaders at mid-market firms (100–1,000 employees).
> **Sender:** Ahmad (founder).
>
> **Placeholders:** `[Name]`, `[Company]`, `[mutual contact]`, `[day/time]`, `[quarter]`.
>
> **Tone guardrails (non-negotiable):**
> - Outcome-led and specific, never hype. No invented metrics or guarantees.
> - No pressure tactics. "Three free slots" is stated as a fact, not "act now / only 2 left."
> - Honest about the exchange: free full access **in return for** feedback + testimonial + case-study rights.
> - Honest about status: this is a **design-partner pilot, not a compliance certification**. End-of-service (EOSB) calculations are still being confirmed with counsel — never present them as validated, certified, or legally confirmed, and never quote an EOSB figure as settled.
> - Templates are drafts — mention "counsel sign-off" where templates come up.
> - Always leave an easy out ("no obligation", "no pressure either way", "happy to leave it with you").
> - EN by default; an optional Arabic line is included inline (Arabic is an official UAE language).

---

## 1. Warm ask for intro (Ahmad → connector)

**Goal:** get the connector to forward a blurb or make an intro. Two parts: the ask, and a forwardable blurb.

### 1a. The ask (~95 words)
> Hi [Name] — quick ask. I'm opening three free pilot slots for Antum People, the UAE onboarding and offboarding tool I've been building. It puts MoHRE steps, PDPL consent capture and bilingual documents in one place, so HR isn't running onboarding and offboarding across spreadsheets and email. If you know an HR or People leader running a 100–1,000 person team in the UAE who's tired of exactly that, would you be open to an intro? I can send you a two-line blurb you forward as-is. No pressure either way.

### 1b. Forwardable blurb (~65 words)
> Antum People helps UAE HR teams (100–1,000 people) run onboarding and offboarding in one place — MoHRE steps, PDPL consent capture, and bilingual documents, instead of spreadsheets and email. We're running three free pilot slots (full access, no cost) in exchange for feedback and a case study. Want a 30-minute look?

## Honest-caveat reminders (for the sender, not pasted into every message)

1. **Pilot is an exchange, not a giveaway.** Always name the ask: feedback + testimonial + case-study rights. Never imply "free forever."
2. **EOSB is not validated.** The UAE EOSB resignation tier is frozen and unconfirmed pending counsel. Never say gratuity is "correct", "accurate", "validated", "certified" or "legally confirmed", and never quote an EOSB figure as settled. If it comes up, say: "the end-of-service numbers are still being confirmed with counsel — we'd validate them against your cases during the pilot."
3. **Templates need counsel sign-off.** If privacy notice / DPA / DPIA come up, say "drafts for your counsel to review" — never "approved."
4. **Analytics are direction, not a shipping feature.** Pre-boarding, probation and workforce-cost intelligence are specced but not built. Describe them as "where we're headed", never "available", "in beta" or "shipping."
5. **No performance guarantees.** We measure outcomes during the pilot; we do **not** promise a specific improvement before we've run it.
6. **Regulator integrations are roadmap.** MoHRE is a workflow step today, not a live API push. Don't overclaim.
7. **Three slots is real, not urgency.** Say "three free slots" as fact; never "only 3 left — act fast."

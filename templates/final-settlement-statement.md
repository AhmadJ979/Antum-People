|---|---|

| **Wage-derived Dues / المستحقات المرتبطة بالأجر** | **[wage_derived_dues]** |
| **End of Service Benefit (EOSB) / مكافأة نهاية الخدمة** | **[eosb_amount]** |
| **Gross Total / إجمالي المستحقات** | **[gross_settlement]** |

The End of Service Benefit is its own entitlement line. No deduction is set off against it: it is
paid in full, whatever is deducted from the wage-derived dues above it.

#### Deductions / الاستقطاعات

| Deduction Type / نوع الاستقطاع | Amount / المبلغ |
|---|---|
| Outstanding Loans / قروض قائمة | [loan_deduction] |
| Assets Not Returned / عهد لم يتم إرجاعها | [assets_deduction] |
| **Total Deductions / إجمالي الاستقطاعات** | **[total_deductions]** |

Deductions are applied to the wage-derived dues only, and may not exceed them. Where the
deductions come to more than those dues they are limited to the dues, and the excess is not
recovered from the End of Service Benefit.

---

### 3. Net Final Payment / صافي المستحق النهائي

**NET PAYABLE AMOUNT: [net_settlement]**

Net payable = End of Service Benefit + (Wage-derived Dues − Total Deductions). The End of Service
Benefit enters the total whole: the deductions are taken from the wage-derived dues alone.

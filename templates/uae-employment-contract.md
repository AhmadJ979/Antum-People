#### 2.7 End of Service Benefits / مكافأة نهاية الخدمة

EOSB shall be calculated in accordance with Articles 51, 52, 53, and 132 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, based on the employee's **basic salary only**.

- **1–5 years:** 21 days' basic salary per year
- **5+ years:** 30 days' basic salary per year
- **Cap:** Maximum 2 years' basic salary (730 days)
- **Resignation reduction:** 1/3 (1–3 yrs), 2/3 (3–5 yrs), full (5+ yrs)
- **Eligibility:** Minimum 1 year of continuous service

Full calculation details are available in the VantageHR EOSB Engine.

### PART 3: GENERAL TERMS / أحكام عامة

#### 3.1 Obligations of the Employee / التزامات الموظف

The Employee agrees to:
1. Perform duties diligently and in accordance with the Employer's policies.
2. Comply with all applicable UAE laws, including labour law and PDPL.
3. Maintain confidentiality of the Employer's trade secrets and business information.
4. Refrain from working for another employer without written consent.
5. Notify the Employer immediately of any change in personal circumstances affecting visa/residency.

#### 3.2 Obligations of the Employer / التزامات صاحب العمل

The Employer agrees to:
1. Pay the salary in full and on time via the Wages Protection System (WPS).
2. Provide a safe working environment in compliance with UAE occupational health and safety regulations.
3. Sponsor the Employee's work visa and residency, and bear all associated costs.
4. Register the Employee with MoHRE and provide a labour card.
5. Provide annual leave, sick leave, and other statutory entitlements.
6. Calculate and pay End-of-Service Benefits in accordance with the law upon termination.

#### 3.3 Termination Grounds / أسباب إنهاء الخدمة

| Ground / السبب | Legal Reference / المرجع القانوني |
|---|---|
| Mutual agreement / الاتفاق المتبادل | Art. 42 |
| Expiry of fixed term / انتهاء المدة المحددة | Art. 8 |
| Resignation by Employee / استقالة الموظف | Art. 43 |
| Termination by Employer (valid reason) / إنهاء من قبل صاحب العمل (سبب مشروع) | Art. 43 |
| Summary dismissal (gross misconduct) / الفصل التأديبي (سوء سلوك جسيم) | Art. 44 |
| Redundancy / الاستغناء عن الخدمة | Art. 45 |
| Constructive dismissal / الإنهاء التعسفي | Art. 47 |

#### 3.4 Dispute Resolution / تسوية المنازعات

Any disputes arising from this contract shall be resolved as follows:

1. **Internal Grievance** — Submit written grievance to HR within 14 days.
2. **MoHRE Conciliation** — File a complaint with MoHRE through the Tas'heel system.
3. **Labour Court** — Refer to the competent UAE Labour Court if conciliation fails.

#### 3.5 Data Protection / حماية البيانات

The Employee acknowledges receipt of the Employer's Privacy Notice (see separate document). The Employer will process the Employee's personal data in accordance with UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and this contract. The Employee consents to the processing of personal data necessary for the performance of this contract and compliance with legal obligations.

#### 3.6 Entire Agreement / الاتفاق الكامل

This contract constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior agreements, whether written or oral. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

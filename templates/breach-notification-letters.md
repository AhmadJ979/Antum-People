# Breach Notification Templates — Antum People

> **Jurisdictions:** UAE (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and KSA (Personal Data Protection Law M/148)
> **Status:** Template v1.1 — Legal-use versions
> **Revision:** Brand updated to Antum People on 2026-09-17.
> **Usage:** Populated by Antum People breach notification workflow, generated as PDF for regulatory submission and email for data subject notification.

---

    [Employee Email]

your data seriously and regret this incident.

WHAT HAPPENED
─────────────
[Brief description of the breach in plain, non-technical language]

WHAT DATA WAS INVOLVED
───────────────────────
[Categories of your personal data that may have been affected]
• [Category 1, e.g., Name, Emirates ID]
• [Category 2, e.g., Salary information]
• [Category 3, e.g., Contact details]

─────────────────

WHAT YOU CAN DO
───────────────
[Recommended actions for the affected individual]
• [Action 1, e.g., Monitor your accounts for suspicious activity]
• [Action 2, e.g., Contact your bank if financial data was involved]
• [Action 3, e.g., Report any concerns to the police/regulator]

WHO TO CONTACT
───────────────
  Data Protection Officer:  [DPO Name]
  Email:                    [DPO Email]
  Phone:                    [DPO Phone]
  Reference Number:         VHR-BR-[YYYYMMDD]-[XXXXX]

  UAE:  UAE Data Office — https://dataoffice.gov.ae

taking all necessary steps to prevent recurrence.

Yours sincerely,

4. Cross-Border Transfers

Your data is stored and processed primarily in [data_residency_country] ([UAE/KSA]). Where a transfer outside the country of origin is necessary:

  • UAE: transfers occur only where the destination ensures an adequate level of protection, or where appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place, per UAE PDPL Art. 22–23 and Cabinet Decision No. 65 of 2022.
  • KSA: transfers occur only where permitted under KSA PDPL Art. 29 (as amended 2023) — e.g., necessity for contract performance, adequate protection in the destination, or a lawful derogation — and, for sensitive data, subject to the conditions set by SDAIA and its Transfer Regulations.
  • Intra-group (UAE ↔ KSA): transfers between our UAE and KSA entities are made under SCCs or an adequacy assessment and are kept segregated by jurisdiction.

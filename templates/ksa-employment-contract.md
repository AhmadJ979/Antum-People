#### 2.7 End of Service Benefits / مكافأة نهاية الخدمة

EOSB shall be calculated in accordance with Article 84 of the Saudi Labor Law (Royal Decree M/51), based on the employee's **total monthly salary**.

| Service Duration | EOSB Per Year of Service |
|---|---|
| First 5 years / أول 5 سنوات | Half a month's total salary per year / نصف شهر من إجمالي الراتب عن كل سنة |
| Each subsequent year / كل سنة تالية | One full month's total salary per year / شهر كامل من إجمالي الراتب عن كل سنة |

> **Penalty for Failure to Give Proper Notice:** Up to 50% of EOSB may be deducted if the employee fails to serve the full notice period.

Full calculation details are available in the Antum People EOSB Engine.

### PART 3: GENERAL TERMS / أحكام عامة

#### 3.1 Obligations of the Employee / التزامات الموظف

The Employee agrees to:
1. Perform duties diligently and in accordance with the Employer's policies.
2. Comply with all applicable KSA laws, including the Labor Law and PDPL.
3. Maintain confidentiality of the Employer's trade secrets and business information.

#### 3.2 Obligations of the Employer / التزامات صاحب العمل

The Employer agrees to:
1. Pay the salary in full and on time via an approved bank transfer (WPS-compliant).
2. Provide a safe working environment in compliance with KSA occupational health and safety regulations.
3. Sponsor the Employee's work visa and Iqama, bearing all associated statutory costs.
4. Provide annual leave, sick leave, and other statutory entitlements.
5. Provide a service certificate within 2 weeks of termination.

#### 3.3 Termination Grounds / أسباب إنهاء الخدمة

| Ground / السبب | Legal Reference / المرجع القانوني |
|---|---|
| Mutual agreement / الاتفاق المتبادل | Art. 74 |
| Expiry of fixed term / انتهاء المدة المحددة | Art. 74 |
| Resignation by Employee | Art. 75, 77 |
| Summary dismissal (gross misconduct) | Art. 80 |
| Force majeure / القوة القاهرة | Art. 82 |

#### 3.4 Dispute Resolution / تسوية المنازعات

Any disputes arising from this contract shall be resolved as follows:

1. **Internal Grievance** — Submit written grievance to HR within 14 days.
2. **MHRSD Conciliation** — File a complaint with the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.
3. **Labor Court** — Refer to the competent KSA Labour Court if conciliation fails.

#### 3.5 Data Protection / حماية البيانات

The Employee acknowledges receipt of the Employer's Privacy Notice (see separate document). The Employer will process the Employee's personal data in accordance with the KSA Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/148) and this contract.

#### 3.6 Entire Agreement / الاتفاق الكامل

This contract constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior agreements. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

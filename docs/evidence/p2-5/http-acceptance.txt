TWO PLACES WHERE THE SPEC'S OWN WORDS AND ITS OWN RULE DO NOT QUITE MEET (reported, not silently
resolved) — see the PR body for the arithmetic
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Section 1 counts whole days to the start date, section 5 measures the boundary in hours. With
   A = days_to_start and t = hours elapsed since 00:00 today, the boundary distance is (A*24 - t)
   hours, so a case with A = 2 sits between 24 and 48 hours out for the whole of that day: its day
   column reads "2 d" while its chip reads "Inside 48 hours · 7 items open". Both numbers are true
   under their own definitions, and they sit on the same row. Implemented as the spec defines both;
   the copy decision is the owner's.
2. Section 5 makes the boundary inclusive at exactly 48.00 h but words the inside-48 headline as
   "start in under 48 hours". At exactly 48.000 hours that sentence is false. The chip label is the
   spec's own text ("Inside 48 hours · N items open"); the headline reads "N items open · start in
   48 hours or less", which is true on both sides of the boundary.
